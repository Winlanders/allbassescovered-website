Our mission

Our approach

At All Basses Covered, we bridge access to quality bass education by providing free resources, mentorship, and support to underrepresented communities. Through school outreach, personalized training, and engaging content on social media, we aim to inspire the next generation of musicians while promoting equity in music education.

Community outreach

We go to you.

Can't come to us? No worries, we'll come to you. Whether it's your school, community center, or a local event, All Basses Covered brings bass support directly to those who need it most. We're committed to meeting students where they are, literally.
